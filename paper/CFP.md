## Submission Instructions & Formatting Guidelines

### Format & Class
- Submissions must follow the formatting guidelines of **Springer’s LNCS** using the `llncs.cls` class (except ESOP, which allows flexible formatting at submission time).
- Submissions must be uploaded in PDF through the EasyChair author interface of the respective conference.
- **Data Availability Statement:** Mandatory for all proceedings papers; placed immediately before the references (does not count toward the page limit).

### Double-Blind Reviewing Policy
- ETAPS conferences use **double-blind reviewing** (for TACAS and iFS, double-blind applies only to *regular research papers*).
- Authors must omit names/institutions, cite own prior work in the third person, and exclude self-identifying acknowledgments.
- Tool papers and tool demonstrations at TACAS are single-blind.

### Page Limits (Excluding Bibliography)
- **TACAS:**
  - Regular research papers, case study papers, regular tool papers: **max 18 pp** (+ optional appendix).
  - Tool demonstration papers: **max 6 pp** (+ optional appendix).
  - *Note:* Each author can submit at most 5 papers to TACAS.
- **iFS:**
  - Long papers (Regular research, Empirical evaluation, Tool papers): **16 pp + 2 pp references**.
  - Short papers (Empirical evaluation, Data showcase, NIER, Tool papers): **8 pp + 1 pp references** (+ optional appendix up to 6 pp for Data showcase / Tool papers).
- **FoSSaCS:**
  - Regular research papers: **max 18 pp**.
- **ESOP:**
  - Regular research papers: **max 25 pp**.
  - Experience reports & Fresh perspectives: **max 15 pp**.

## Artifact Submission & Evaluation

- **TACAS Tool Papers:** Regular tool paper and tool demonstration paper submissions to TACAS **must** be accompanied by an artifact submitted shortly after the paper (by Oct 29, 2026). The artifact outcome will be considered in the acceptance decision.
- **Research & Case Study Papers (TACAS, ESOP, iFS, FoSSaCS):** Participation in artifact evaluation is voluntary after paper acceptance.
- **Publication:** Published in the *Advanced Research in Computing and Software Science (ARCoSS)* subline of Springer’s LNCS series in **Gold Open Access** (CC-BY).
